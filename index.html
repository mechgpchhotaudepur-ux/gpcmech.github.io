Mechanical Engineering Department

The Mechanical Department of Government Polytechnic Chhotaudepur is one of the most prominent and sought-after departments of the institute. It offers a three-year diploma program in Mechanical Engineering that provides students with a solid foundation in the principles and practices of mechanical engineering.

Well-Equipped Labs & Workshop

The department has a well-equipped laboratory with state-of-the-art machinery and equipment. The lab is used for practical sessions and experiments that complement the theoretical concepts taught in the classroom.

Students also have access to a workshop where they can work on projects and gain hands-on experience.

menu_bookCurriculum

From Fundamentals to CAD/CAM

The curriculum is designed to equip students with the knowledge and skills required to excel in industry. It covers Mechanics, Materials Science, Thermodynamics, Manufacturing Processes and Machine Design.

Students also learn Computer-Aided Design (CAD) and Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM), essential tools in modern mechanical engineering.

AREA 01Mechanics

Statics, dynamics and the behaviour of machine elements under load.

AREA 02Materials Science

Engineering materials, their properties and testing.

AREA 03Thermodynamics

Energy, heat and the thermal cycles behind engines and refrigeration.

AREA 04Manufacturing

Machining, welding, foundry and forming processes.

AREA 05Machine Design

Designing components and mechanisms that are safe and efficient.

AREA 06CAD & CAM

Computer-aided design, drafting and computer-aided manufacturing.
