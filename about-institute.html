About the Institute

Government Polytechnic Chhotaudepur is one of the leading institutes in diploma technical education. The institute runs four diploma courses (Mechanical, Electrical, Civil and Automobile) under the Commissionerate of Technical Education (CTE), is affiliated with Gujarat Technological University and is approved by AICTE.

Established in 1994, the institute has some of the best infrastructure in Chhotaudepur. Located on the city's main highway, it is a first choice among students for admission.
