Rules & Regulations

Study, discipline, attendance and GTU detention rules that every student must follow.

gavelStudy & Discipline

  • All students are required to enrol at Gujarat Technological University by filling in the prescribed form available at the student section. Students must abide by the rules and regulations, along with instructions issued by the college authorities from time to time.
  • No student will indulge in activities like ragging, misbehaviour or indiscipline, or help in promoting them. Students found indulging in any such activity will be immediately expelled from college.
  • Students are instructed to act and behave in a way that dignifies the name of the college in academics, sports, social service and the economic development of the nation, so that one can be proud of being a student of this institute.

event_availableAttendance Rule

  • Minimum 75% attendance is required for filling in the GTU examination form and to appear in the End Semester or External Examination.
  • 10% of total absence due to illness may be considered by the institute (Circular No: GTU/Circular/M.L./2011/2763, Date: 02-04-2011).
  • ExampleIf you are absent for 20 days due to illness, then only 2 days will be considered as present.

ruleGTU Detention Rules

  • Students having more than 4 backlogs, excluding the immediate previous semester's backlogs, are not eligible to continue their studies until they fulfil the eligibility criteria.
  • Immediate previous semester backlogs are not counted for detention purposes.
  • ExampleSuppose you have 2 backlogs in Semester I, 1 in Semester II and 3 in Semester III, and you are studying in Semester IV. Total backlogs = 6 (2+1+3), but for detention only 3 (2+1) are counted, so you are not detained. When you reach Semester V, all backlogs before Semester IV are counted, and if you have not cleared any of them, you are detained.
